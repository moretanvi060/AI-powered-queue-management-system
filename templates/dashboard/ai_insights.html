📐 How the AI Prediction & Optimization Engine Calculates

1. Waiting Time Modeling

$$\text{Estimated Wait} = \frac{\text{Queue Depth} \times \text{Avg Service Duration}}{\text{Active Counters} \times \text{Counter Efficiency}} \times \text{Crowd Friction}$$ Confidence rating recalculates dynamically on each token dispatch.

2. Bottleneck Detection

When queue length exceeds $5 \times \text{Active Counters}$ or wait time surpasses 25 minutes, the system triggers proactive counter recommendations with calculated % reduction.

3. Anti-Starvation Priority

Weighted priority queuing with time aging ($+25\text{ pts/min}$) ensures emergency and senior cases are prioritized while standard walk-ins never experience starvation.
